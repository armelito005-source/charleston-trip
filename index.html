Our Charleston long weekend

Suggested day by day

A gentle path through the weekend. Tap hearts on cards below to save favorites for later.

Thursday · Arrive

Oct 15
  1. 4:00p
    Check in at the Hanahan condo (after 4 PM).
  2. 5:00p
    The Hidden House downtown for a wine flight. Happy hour Wed-Sat 4-6 (confirm hours).
  3. 6:30p
    Dinner at Leon's (walk-in) or stay close at Cane Pazzo in Hanahan (6 min).
  4. 8:00p
    Stroll Waterfront Park, the lit Pineapple Fountain, and Rainbow Row.

Spooky alt: Cane Pazzo at 5:30, then Halloween in the Swamp at Cypress Gardens at 7:30 ($5 or $30, buy online ahead).

Friday · Golf + swamp

Oct 16
  1. 8:00a
    9 holes at Crowfield in Goose Creek ($32/person with cart). Wescott is the backup ($35, rentals $40).
  2. 11:00a
    Lunch near Goose Creek or Moncks Corner.
  3. 12:30p
    Cypress Gardens - self-guided swamp boat (included), Butterfly House, Swamparium, The Notebook spot. Last boats 4:15. $10/adult.
  4. 6:00p
    Dinner at Jackrabbit Filly (reserve) or MOMO on the Cooper River.

Saturday · Shark teeth

Oct 17
  1. Morning
    Sorelle Mercato coffee on Broad St, then Battery / Rainbow Row. Skip Upper King 9-11a (Pride Parade).
  2. Late am
    Angel Oak (free), then lunch toward Folly.
  3. 3:30-6:30
    Morris Island on the falling tide. Tideline shelling stop $55/pp is the budget landing option.
  4. ~6:30p
    Bowens Island for steamed oysters at sunset.

Tide tip: Saturday morning is a rising tide (low ~6:38a, high ~1:25p). Best shark-tooth window is late afternoon on the falling tide, about 4:00-7:45 PM. Ask your boat operator which slot exposes the most beach.

Option B: Dawn beach hunt at Folly right after low tide, then Boathouse at Breach Inlet for rooftop sunset, or City Market Night Market + a ghost tour.

Sunday · Pumpkins & out

Oct 18
  1. ~9:30a
    Checkout by 10 AM. Drive to Boone Hall Farms (Hwy 17 entrance).
  2. 10-noon
    Boone Hall Pumpkin Patch - $15/person. Hayride, corn maze, pig races.
  3. Afternoon
    Ravenel Bridge walk, Shem Creek boardwalk, or one last Sorelle coffee (open until 4).
