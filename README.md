# Charleston Trip Planner

A phone-first visual trip planner for **Armel & Kim** — Charleston / Lowcountry, Thu Oct 15 - Sun Oct 18, 2026.

**Live:** https://armelito005-source.github.io/charleston-trip/

## What's inside

- Stay (Hanahan waterfront condo)
- Golf (Crowfield + Wescott — both labeled **Public**)
- Coffee & wine (Hidden House, Sorelle Mercato)
- Nature & day trips (Cypress Gardens, Boone Hall Pumpkin Patch, Morris Island shark teeth, Angel Oak, Folly Pier)
- Dinner (Cane Pazzo, Jackrabbit Filly, MOMO, Leon's, Bowens Island, Boathouse at Breach Inlet)
- Free & cheap (Waterfront Park, Shem Creek, Ravenel Bridge, City Market Night Market, Riverfront Park)
- Suggested Thu-Sun day-by-day plan
- Save/heart favorites (localStorage)
